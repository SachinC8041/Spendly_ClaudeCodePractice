Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text). As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you add — amount, category, date, and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide the Service to you: authenticating your account, displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see on your dashboard. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database for as long as your account remains active. If you wish to have your account and associated data deleted, you may contact us using the details below.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services. Any infrastructure providers we rely on to host the Service (such as our hosting or database provider) may process data on our behalf strictly to keep the Service running, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
